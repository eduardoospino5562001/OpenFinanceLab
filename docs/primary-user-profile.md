
# OpenFinanceLab - Primary User Profile

## Primary User 
- Small business owners and operators without dedicated financial or data specialists

User Description:

The initial version is intended for small business owners who manage their own financial operations and rely on multiple disconnected platforms, such as QuickBooks, Stripe, banking services, payroll systems, and spreadsheets. These users may have limited financial or technical expertise and struggle to consolidate their information into a clear picture of their business's financial health.

## Financial Data Needs:

- The primary user needs a centralized view of their business's financial information, including:

**Cash Flow:** 
- Monitor bank balances, deposits, withdrawals, and projected cash availability.

**Revenue:** 
- Track sales, transactions, invoices, refunds, and revenue trends.

**Expenses:** 
- Identify operating costs, payroll, subscriptions, and supplier payments.

**Profitability:** 
- Monitor revenue, expenses, profit margins, and financial performance.

**Accounts Receivable/Payable:** 
- Track outstanding invoices, unpaid bills, and upcoming payment obligations.

**Payroll:** 
- Keep track of employee wages, payroll expenses, and associated labor costs

## Primary Challenge:

- Financial information and data are fragmented across multiple platforms,
making it difficult to consolidate, analyze, and obtain a clear picture of 
business performance.

## Primary Goal:

- Provide small business owners with a centralized platform that consolidates their financial data and makes it easier to understand their financial position, identify potential issues, and make informed business decisions.
